## Episode-08

- **transgression**
  - But she knew the transgressions of her past were nothing compared with the sin she was about to commit.
  - **n.** the act of breaking a rule, law, or moral standard
- **D.U.I.**
  - driving under the influence
- **conspicuous**
  - Not since my own suicide had violence intruded upon the serenity of life on Wisteria Lane, in such a conspicuous fashion.
  - **adj.** easy to notice; clearly visible, often in a way that attracts attention.
- **solvent**
  - Lynette brought the broom, Susan brought the trash bags, and Bree brought the industrial strength solvent.
  - **n.** a liquid that dissolves other substances
  - **adj.** having enough money to pay all debts
- **debauchery**
  - I can put up with your debauchery and your food theft, but I will not tolerate spying.
  - **n.** excessive indulgence in sensual pleasures; immoral or wild behaviour.
- **babbling brook**
  - a small stream that makes a soft, gentle murmuring sound as the water flows over stones.
- **judiciously**
  - Promise me you're gonna use it judiciously.
  - **adv.** in a wise, careful, and sensible way; with good judgment.
- **enormity**
  - We need to show Andrew the enormity of what he's done.
  - **n.** the great seriousness and shock of something morally wrong

## Episode-09

- **treachery**

  - **n.** the act of betraying someone who trusts you; betrayal.
- **compassion**
  - And sometimes the only way to ward off the darkness is to shine the light of compassion.
  - **n.** a strong feeling of sympathy and sadness for the suffering of others, and a wish to help them.

## Episode-10

- **villain**

- **fornicate**
  - Daddy's gonna fornicate for us!

- **impounding**
  - The government's impounding it. Here's your receipt.
  - **n.** to take and keep something officially, usually by legal authority.
- **brazen**
  - You know you are pretty brazen for a woman who just admitted, however tacitly, that she just slept with my husband.
  - **n.** shameless and bold; done openly without any embarrassment or fear of punishment.
- **eviscerate**
  - I am gonna go out and find the most vindictive lawyer I can find, and together we are going to eviscerate you.
